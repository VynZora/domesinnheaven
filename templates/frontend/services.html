Know Before You Go

Resort Policies & Guidelines

01

Resort Policies

  • Check-in time is 2:00 PM and Check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and additional charges.
  • It is mandatory to present a valid government-issued photo ID for Indian citizens. All other nationalities must present a valid Passport and Visa at check-in.
  • Pets are not allowed inside the resort.
  • Photo-shoots can be done on a chargeable basis only.
  • All rooms are strictly non-smoking.
  • In-room dining is not available.
  • Loud music is not permitted in public areas. We prioritize maintaining a peaceful environment for all guests and the surrounding nature.
  • Visitors are not allowed inside the resort premises.
02

Child Policy

  • Two children up to 5 years of age can stay free of charge in the parents' room when using the existing bed. A maximum of one extra bed can be provided on a chargeable basis.
  • A child aged 6–12 years, a child above 12 years, or an extra adult will be charged additionally.
03

Cancellation & No-Show Policy

  • In case of no-show or cancellation of a guaranteed reservation, the resort reserves the right to levy a retention charge for the entire stay.
  • 50% retention charges apply for cancellations made within 14 days of arrival. Full retention charges apply for cancellations within 7 days of arrival.
  • Online payment fees and bank transaction charges will be levied on any refund processed within the eligible cancellation period.
