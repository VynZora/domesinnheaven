The Story Behind Domes Inn Heaven

Domes Inn Heaven was created for people who want more from a hill escape than a room and a view. Our geodesic domes bring you closer to the hills while giving you the privacy and comfort of a carefully designed stay. From mist drifting across the valley in the morning to the scent of cardamom in the air, the surroundings are part of the experience here. We keep the pace slow, the setting peaceful and the focus firmly on enjoying Kerala's natural beauty. For travellers searching for a unique stay in Munnar, our approach is simple: give nature the space to be enjoyed without giving up the comforts that make a holiday feel special. It's this balance that makes Domes Inn Heaven a distinctive luxury nature retreat in Kerala.

Our mission is to offer guests a stay that is deeply connected to nature, without sacrificing comfort or style. We believe that luxury and sustainability can coexist, and every decision we make — from the materials we use to the meals we serve — reflects that belief. We exist to remind our guests that the earth, at its finest, is the ultimate luxury.

We envision a future where travellers choose slow, meaningful escapes over rushed tourism. Domes Inn Heaven aspires to be Kerala's most celebrated glamping destination — a place where every guest leaves not just rested, but genuinely transformed by the beauty of what surrounds them.
