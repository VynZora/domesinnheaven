Who We Are

Why Escape to Domes Inn Heaven?

The idea behind Domes Inn Heaven was simple: give guests a chance to experience Munnar from a more personal and unusual setting. The first thought was the dome itself, a space where you could wake up surrounded by nature and look up at the sky at night.

Over time, that idea grew into six unique dome stays: Mercury, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, Uranus and Neptune. Each offers a spacious 550 sq. ft. interior with a king-size latex memory foam bed, air conditioning, flat-screen TV, free Wi-Fi, minibar and pantry, 24-hour hot water, an outdoor jacuzzi and hammock.

We believe some of the best holiday moments are the quiet ones: a cool morning, a warm cup of tea, mist moving across the hills. That is the feeling we want Domes Inn Heaven to hold onto.
